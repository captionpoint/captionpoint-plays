Act I Scene i

The Great Green Room.  A bed-sitting room, a little too large to be cozy.  Tall, curtained windows flank a fireplace UC.  Stars glitter in a clear night sky outside.  Candlesticks and an ormolu clock grace the mantel piece.  Andirons sit on either side of the hearth, a rack of fireplace tools sits left, and a caddy of firewood sits to the right.  Over the fireplace is a large painting of The Cow Jumping Over the Moon in an ornate gilt frame.  It has an air of mystery, of magic potential.

Panning from audience left to right are:  A large doll house extreme left.  UCR is a yellow rocking chair with a knitting project and a ball of yarn on the seat.  UR is a four-self book case.  On top of the case sits a boy doll and a toy giraffe.  On the top shelf sit a row of red books, with one green book open—a copy of The Runaway Bunny.  Blue and green books on the second shelf, red and blue books on the third shelf, all green books on the bottom shelf, plus a toy elephant and a girl rag doll.  Above the bookcase on the wall is an illustration from The Runaway Bunny—mother bunny fishing in the river with a pole baited with a carrot, and baby bunny trying to swim away.

Upstage right of center, midway between the rocker and the fireplace, sits a laundry drying rack, with a pair of socks and a pair of mittens hung up to dry with clothespins.

A large circular braided rug lies center.  

UR is a small circular table with a lamp; laid on the table are a bowl of mush with spoon, a comb, and a brush labeled ‘bunny’.  Above the table floats a red balloon.

Up center right, the bed, with headboard and a blanket triangular folded USMC style in a triangle at the foot.  Directly DS own of the bed—a pair of slippers and a rug of indeterminate animal origin.  Below the bed DR is a small two-drawer dresser.  On top sits another clock, a telephone, and a green copy of ‘Good Night Moon.’  Above the bed is another painting—The Three Bears, sitting in a room.

Both clocks read 7:00 PM.
